🍲 Easy Udon Hot Pot

One pot · pre-cut veggies · sliced meat · udon · about 20 minutes

1) Pick your meat

2) What veggies do you have?

Broth — easy savory version

• 4 cups water or chicken/dashi broth
• 3 tbsp soy sauce
• 2 tbsp mirin (or 1 tbsp sugar if you don't have mirin)
• 1 tsp minced garlic
• Optional: 1 tsp grated/frozen ginger
• Optional: 1 tsp sesame oil at the end

Taste after it comes to a simmer. Add another splash of soy sauce if it tastes too weak.

Your cooking order

Default step-by-step

1
Make the broth.
Add broth ingredients to a medium/large pot and bring to a gentle boil.
2
Add hard vegetables first.
Carrots, daikon, squash, thick cabbage stems: simmer 4–6 min.
3
Add mushrooms + onions.
Simmer about 2–3 min.
4
Add the meat.
Spread thin slices apart so they don't clump. Use a food thermometer; timing alone does not establish safe doneness. Beef and pork whole cuts: 145°F / 63°C, then rest 3 minutes. Chicken: 165°F / 74°C.
5
Add udon.
Frozen or vacuum-packed udon usually needs only 1–3 min. Separate gently with chopsticks/tongs.
6
Add leafy vegetables last.
Bok choy, napa leaves, spinach: 1–2 min, just until tender.
7
Finish + eat.
Taste broth. Add soy sauce if needed. Finish with green onion and optional sesame oil.
Hot-pot trick: don't overcook everything at once. Once the noodles and leafy veggies are tender, lower the heat and eat right from the pot/bowls.
