How to make a ChatGPT Skill

A fast guide to Skills, Projects, and GPTs — plus a reusable prompt to build your own workflow.

Fastest path

1
Open SkillsIn a workspace where Skills are enabled, open the Skills area from ChatGPT.
2
Choose CreateStart a new personal or workspace Skill.
3
Describe the repeatable jobExplain what goes in, what ChatGPT should do, and what the output should look like.
4
Add rules + examplesSpecify required steps, quality checks, templates, and things it must never invent.
5
Install and testTry it on 2–3 realistic examples and tighten the instructions where results vary.

Important availability note

Skills availability depends on your ChatGPT plan and workspace configuration. If you don’t see Skills, a Project or custom GPT can reproduce most of the same behavior.

Skill = reusable workflow Project = ongoing context GPT = specialized assistant

What are you trying to build?

Use a Skill.
Best when the process should be consistent every time, regardless of the conversation.
Use a Project.
Best when the files, decisions, history, and context should persist around one initiative.
Use a custom GPT.
Best when you want a purpose-built assistant with its own identity, knowledge, and tool setup.
