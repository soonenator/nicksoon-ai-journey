Why I didn't just use all six favorites

Blastoise: absolutely worth building around. Mega Blastoise has a real tournament shell-smash niche, and Water Spout + Dark Pulse is the standard offensive core.

Raichu: a strong doubles support choice even without Mega Evolving. Lightning Rod is especially valuable beside Blastoise.

Snorlax: viable as a bulky slow-mode piece, but it needs Trick Room support to feel threatening rather than passive.

Heracross: dangerous, but the 4× Flying weakness is a real liability and Mega Heracross would take the Mega slot away from Blastoise.

Decidueye: usable but fringe in M‑B. It brings nice Grass/Ghost coverage, yet doesn't solve as many team problems as Farigiraf/Sinistcha.

Pikachu: Light Ball is fun, but Raichu gives you a cleaner support profile.
