My route
If I were hunting today: Target → Best Buy → GameStop → Barnes & Noble. Only after that would I check local card shops for sealed product—unless you’re shopping for singles.
Prices above are useful benchmarks for recent standard products; special sets and premium collections can have different MSRP. Availability changes quickly, so tap the retailer links before driving over.
