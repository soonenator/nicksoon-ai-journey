# Art and attribution

An unofficial fan-inspired prototype, not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney or the creators of Vampire Survivors.

The character atlas, modular robe body, desert key art and arena paintings were originally generated for this prototype and are bundled in `assets/`. Character articulation, effects, enemy behavior and stage geometry are implemented in the game code. No assets or source code were copied from Vampire Survivors or Poketide.

General survival-game inspiration: https://poncle.games/vampire-survivors

Fonts: DM Sans and Barlow Condensed via Google Fonts, with system-font fallbacks.
